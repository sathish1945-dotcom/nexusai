NexusAI

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026. NexusAI provides AI chat and optional Google Workspace connections.

Data we process

We store account details, hashed passwords, encrypted integration credentials, connection metadata, usage counters and security records. When you request a Google feature, the app retrieves the relevant Gmail, Calendar or Drive data with your permission. Tool parameters and results may be stored in audit logs and execution records. Chat history may also be saved in your browser.

AI processing and service providers

Chat messages and requested tool results may be sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider to produce a response. Hosting and database services process application data. Avoid submitting information you do not want processed by these services. Provider retention and processing depend on their applicable terms and configuration.

Google permissions

Google connections request read-only access for the selected features. You can decline access. NexusAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Disconnecting removes stored integration credentials and requests revocation from Google. It does not automatically erase account records, chat history, audit logs or previous execution results. You can also remove access in your Google Account permissions. Contact us to request deletion of retained account data. No fixed automatic retention period is currently implemented for audit and execution records.

Security and contact

Integration credentials are encrypted on the server. No system can guarantee absolute security. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact psathishkani@gmail.com.
